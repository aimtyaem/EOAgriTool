Extension recommendations

Immediate: inoculate soybean with locally adapted Bradyrhizobium at sowing and inspect nodulation at V3–V4.

Soil recovery: incorporate berseem biomass at 50–60 days; retain crop residues and reduce tillage.

Fertility: band 30–40 kg P₂O₅/ha. Monitor iron and zinc; use Fe-EDDHA or ZnSO₄ only where symptoms or tests justify application.

Water: maintain a leaching fraction of at least 15% while avoiding over-irrigation and capillary salt rise.
